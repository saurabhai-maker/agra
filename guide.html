Connectivity & High-Speed Rail

How to Reach Agra from Delhi & Beyond

Agra is connected via world-class high-speed express trains and the 6-lane Yamuna Expressway.

🚄

Gatimaan Express (Train #12050)

India’s premium semi-high speed train operating at 160 km/h. Departs Delhi Hazrat Nizamuddin at 08:10 AM and arrives at Agra Cantt in just 100 minutes (09:50 AM). Fully air-conditioned with onboard catering.

Top Pick for Day Trips
🚇

New Agra Metro (Priority Corridor)

Connects Taj East Gate directly to Agra Fort / Mankameshwar Metro Station. Fast, air-conditioned, token-based ticketing (₹20–₹30), runs from 06:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily.

Traffic-Free Sightseeing
🛣️

Yamuna Expressway (Private Cab)

Modern 6-lane access-controlled highway from Greater Noida to Agra. Driving time is approximately 2.5 to 3 hours. Ideal for families and groups traveling with door-to-door luggage.

Strict Compliance Guidelines

Essential Rules for Visiting The Taj Mahal

Avoid delays at security checkpoints by reviewing these official Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) regulations.

🚫 Prohibited Items Inside Gates

Tripods, camera drones, large rucksacks, food items, tobacco, cigarettes, lighters, and knives are strictly barred. Small water bottles and mobile phones/cameras are allowed. Cloakrooms are available at gates.

⚠️ Friday Weekly Closure

The Taj Mahal is closed every Friday for general viewing. However, Agra Fort, Mehtab Bagh, and Fatehpur Sikri remain fully open on Fridays. Always check your travel calendar before booking train tickets.

📷 Photography Etiquette

Photography is freely permitted across the gardens, reflective pool, and outer plinth. However, photography is strictly prohibited inside the main interior burial chamber. Silence must be observed inside.

🛡️ Verified Guides Only

Only hire guides wearing official photo ID cards issued by the Ministry of Tourism / Archaeological Survey of India (ASI). Decline unsolicited offers from street touts outside the security zone.

  • Oct – Mar: Peak season, cool pleasant weather (10°–25°C).
